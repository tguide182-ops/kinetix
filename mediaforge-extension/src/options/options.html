      Privacy & diagnostics

      MediaForge works entirely on your device. It never sends your browsing history, detected media URLs or page contents to any server, and it never reads or stores cookies, passwords or authorization headers. Detected media lists live in memory for the current browser session only.

      DRM-protected or encrypted media, and media behind access controls you cannot otherwise reach, are intentionally unsupported.
